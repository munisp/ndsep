# Lagos Procurement Presentation Script

This script is tailored for a Lagos State audience that includes **state land agencies, executive procurement reviewers, urban development stakeholders, and builders**. The tone should remain formal, practical, and results-oriented. The core message is that the platform helps Lagos move from fragmented land and approval workflows to a more visible, parcel-driven, and revenue-aware operating model.

## Slide 1 — Title

Open by positioning the platform as a procurement-grade operating environment for Lagos State. Explain that it brings together parcel intelligence, real web geospatial review, Certificate of Occupancy progression, AI-assisted intake, field continuity, and audit trust in one platform. State that Lagos is not simply procuring software; it is procuring delivery discipline for land administration.

## Slide 2 — Lagos Service and Land-Administration Challenge

Frame the problem in Lagos terms. Emphasize development intensity, title regularisation pressure, frequent coordination between land, planning, infrastructure, and private development actors, and the public cost of opaque or slow workflows. The issue is not only delay; it is the loss of visibility over service quality, public confidence, and land-linked fiscal value.

## Slide 3 — Real Geospatial Evidence

Use the full-screen parcel map as a proof point. Explain that the map is a real web screenshot from the live product and that it shows multiple Nigerian parcel markers, corridor overlays, and parcel intelligence in one workspace. For Lagos, stress how this supports housing growth review, corridor-sensitive approvals, right-of-way conflict detection, and desk-based parcel triage before field escalation.

## Slide 4 — C of O Workflow Continuity

This should be one of the strongest slides. Explain that Lagos needs clear visibility from application intake through review, approval, signing, registration, and post-title continuity. The platform makes that progression visible and auditable, helping officers, applicants, and leadership understand where cases are moving, where they are delayed, and where intervention is needed.

## Slide 5 — Field and Corridor Operations

Explain that dense growth environments still require physical verification. Show how mission workflows, offline continuity, evidence capture, replay, and secure audit-package handling help Lagos field teams continue inspections, corridor checks, and dispute-sensitive verification even when connectivity is unstable.

## Slide 6 — AI-Assisted Intake and Review

Present AI as a throughput tool rather than a replacement for officers. Explain that uploaded PDFs and images can be parsed to prefill case records, while side-by-side verification keeps human reviewers in control. For Lagos, the value is faster intake, less repetitive clerical work, and more consistent review quality at scale.

## Slide 7 — Revenue Assurance and Builder Value

Address monetization directly. Explain that Lagos captures value through stronger formalisation visibility, more traceable approvals, reduced leakage, and better alignment between service delivery and state value capture. Builders capture value through shorter approval uncertainty, lower idle capital costs, better project planning, and stronger buyer trust.

## Slide 8 — Rollout Path

Close with implementation credibility. Recommend a phased Lagos rollout beginning with land administration and C of O continuity, then extending to parcel map review, corridor overlays, builder-facing workflows, and executive revenue dashboards. The closing message is that Lagos can start where pressure is highest and expand without replacing the platform foundation.
