## Cover
**IDLR-PTS Platform**

Nigeria’s integrated platform for land, permits, geospatial intelligence, field operations, evidence trust, and economic delivery

Prepared for federal institutions, state agencies, builders, NGOs, and end buyers

## Slide 1
**Why Nigeria needs this now**

Nigeria faces overlapping pressure in land administration, housing delivery, extractives governance, and public service efficiency.

| Pressure point | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Nigeria needs **550,000 housing units annually** | Slow approvals directly affect housing supply and affordability [1] |
| Land formalisation remains below **10%**, with ambition to reach **50% in 10 years** | Weak records limit investment, mortgages, and state revenue [1] |
| The mining cadastre already manages **5,000+ mineral titles** and **2,000+ applications annually** | High-volume approvals require transparent workflow and trusted records [2] |

## Slide 2
**What the platform really is**

This is not only a permitting dashboard. It is a Nigeria-ready operating system for land-linked approvals, evidence, field validation, audit integrity, and multi-agency public delivery.

| Platform layer | What it does in Nigeria |
|---|---|
| Land and parcel intelligence | Links permits, assets, parcel context, and inspections |
| Approval orchestration | Coordinates mining, oil and gas, planning, environment, and land workflows |
| Field operations | Supports low-connectivity inspections, alerts, and secure offline access |
| AI and document intelligence | Extracts, verifies, summarizes, and prioritizes operational information |
| Audit and trust controls | Produces verifiable, tamper-evident records and custody history |

## Slide 9
**Low-connectivity field operations are a strategic strength**

| Field capability | Why it matters in Nigeria |
|---|---|
| Offline audit-package caching | Inspectors can carry evidence into weak-connectivity environments |
| Encrypted local cache | Sensitive materials remain protected on device |
| Biometric unlock | Access to offline evidence is controlled for field staff |
| Offline replay queue | Field events persist locally until service returns |
| Conflict reconciliation | Duplicate or delayed geofence events are resolved safely |
| Notification-driven field coordination | Field and office teams stay aligned on exceptions and deadlines |
