## Cover
**IDLR-PTS Platform**

A unified permitting, land workflow, compliance, and audit platform for Nigeria and Africa

Prepared for federal and state institutions, builders, NGOs, and end buyers

## Slide 1
**Why this matters now**

Nigeria faces a scale problem that cannot be solved with fragmented approvals, paper-heavy workflows, and disconnected agency records.

| Pressure on the system | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Nigeria needs **550,000 housing units annually** to curb the housing deficit | Delayed approvals directly slow housing delivery and access to formal shelter [1] |
| Land formalisation is still below **10%**, with a national ambition to reach **50% in 10 years** | Weak land records suppress investment, tax capture, and mortgage readiness [1] |
| Nigeria’s mining cadastre sees **5,000+ mineral titles** and processes **2,000+ applications annually** | High-volume approvals need transparent, rules-based workflow and audit integrity [2] |

## Slide 2

**Why this can scale across Africa**

The underlying problem is continental: fragmented land records, overlapping approvals, weak evidence traceability, low-connectivity field work, and trust gaps between public institutions and market actors. A platform designed for Nigeria’s complexity is well positioned for other African jurisdictions facing similar coordination and formalisation challenges.
