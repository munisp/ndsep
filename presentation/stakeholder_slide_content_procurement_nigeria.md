# Procurement-Grade Nigeria Deck Outline

## Positioning
This version is designed for procurement, policy, and executive review. It emphasizes state land administration modernization, Certificate of Occupancy workflow continuity, revenue assurance, public trust, and implementation credibility, while still showing geospatial intelligence, AI-assisted document extraction, low-connectivity field operations, and audit integrity.

## Proposed slide structure

### Slide 1 — Title
Position the platform as Nigeria’s integrated land, approvals, and public-delivery operating system.

### Slide 2 — Nigeria’s service-delivery and revenue problem
Show how fragmented approvals, weak land formalisation, and disconnected field evidence slow service, reduce internally generated revenue, and weaken trust.

### Slide 3 — What government is actually procuring
Frame the platform as operating infrastructure for parcel intelligence, Certificate of Occupancy workflows, approvals, AI-assisted intake, field execution, and trusted audit.

### Slide 4 — Geospatial and parcel intelligence
Use real Parcels and Geo screenshots and explicitly label them as real web parcel-intelligence views, with a note that the richer live map layer is native-map-dependent.

### Slide 5 — Certificate of Occupancy and land-rights workflow continuity
Use the real legal workflow screenshot to show C of O, Right of Occupancy, Governor Consent, review, signing, and registration progression.

### Slide 6 — Low-connectivity field operations
Show field mission workflow, secure cache, offline replay, geofence signals, and biometric-gated audit access.

### Slide 7 — AI-assisted document extraction and review
Explain uploaded PDF and image parsing, form prefilling, side-by-side verification, field provenance, and reviewer control.

### Slide 8 — Multi-agency governance and approval queues
Show role-based queues, timed handoffs, supervisor overrides, reassignment, reminder scheduling, and digest summaries.

### Slide 9 — Audit integrity, verification, and trust controls
Show audit verification, public-key validation, signing-key rotation, revocation, chain of custody, and downloadable audit packages.

### Slide 10 — Revenue assurance and monetization
Show how states and government capture value through formalisation, fee traceability, approval-cycle compression, lower leakage, and stronger land-value capture, while builders capture value through lower delay cost and faster conversion.

### Slide 11 — Stakeholder value by audience
Compare value for federal regulators, state land agencies, builders, with supporting relevance for NGOs, lenders, and end buyers.

### Slide 12 — Procurement and rollout case
Close with implementation credibility, portability, self-hosting readiness, cross-surface delivery, and why this is a scalable Nigeria-first platform.
